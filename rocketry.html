High Power Rocketry

Team-Based Rocket Design & Propulsion Systems

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a team-focused high power rocketry effort centered on the design, fabrication, and testing of a competition-class rocket. The objective was to safely achieve a targeted altitude while meeting structural, propulsion, and recovery requirements.

While this was a collaborative effort, my contributions focused on propulsion-related components, structural integration, and hands-on fabrication support throughout the build process.

Technical Contributions

  • Assisted in propulsion system integration and motor selection
  • Supported airframe assembly and structural reinforcement
  • Participated in recovery system installation and ground testing
  • Contributed to pre-launch preparation and safety checks

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of propulsion systems, structural design tradeoffs, and team-based engineering workflows. It also reinforced the importance of communication, safety, and documentation in aerospace projects.
