High Power Rocketry

Team-Based Rocket Design, Fabrication, and Testing

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a high power rocketry team focused on designing and building a competition-ready rocket. The goal was to achieve reliable flight performance while meeting strict safety and structural requirements.

Although this was a team effort, my contributions focused on propulsion-related components, structural manufacturing, and hands-on integration during the build and testing phases.

CAD & Design (Onshape)

I contributed to the CAD development of rocket components using Onshape, supporting iterative design changes for fins, motor mounts, and structural elements.

Version control and real-time collaboration allowed the team to quickly implement changes and maintain consistency across subsystems.

Manufacturing & Materials

I supported manufacturing through 3D printing, fiberglass layups, and laser cutting. These processes were used to produce fins, internal structures, and reinforcement components designed to withstand high aerodynamic and thermal loads.

Material selection prioritized strength-to-weight ratio, heat resistance, and structural reliability near the propulsion system.

Integration & Testing

I assisted in final assembly, propulsion system integration, and recovery system installation. Pre-launch checks and ground testing ensured structural integrity and safe deployment mechanisms.

This phase emphasized precision, safety protocols, and cross-team coordination.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of propulsion integration, composite manufacturing, and team-based engineering workflows. It reinforced the importance of iterative design, testing, and clear technical communication.
