Unmanned Drone Prototyping

Hands-on UAV Design, Assembly, and Flight Testing

Project Overview

I participated in a 9-week unmanned drone prototyping course where I gained hands-on experience designing, building, and testing UAVs. This course emphasized teamwork, troubleshooting, and iterative aerospace system design.

Drone Kit Assembly

I built a functional UAV from a kit by assembling the frame, mounting motors and ESCs, and ensuring proper mechanical alignment to prevent vibration and instability during flight.

I also hand-soldered the power and signal connections between the flight controller, receivers, and servos to ensure reliable electrical performance.

ArduPilot Configuration & Flight Control

In a group setting, we configured our UAV using ArduPilot software. This included:

  • Frame configuration and motor calibration
  • Establishing reliable RC communication links
  • Servo calibration for precise control
  • Programming flight modes (Stabilize, AltHold, Autonomous)

We validated performance through bench tests, indoor trials, and outdoor flight testing.

Results & Skills Gained

  • UAV mechanical and electrical assembly
  • Flight controller configuration and tuning
  • Soldering and wiring best practices
  • Team-based engineering problem solving
  • Flight testing and troubleshooting
