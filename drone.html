Unmanned Drone Prototyping

Hands-on UAV Design, Assembly, and Flight Testing

Project Overview

Participated in a 9-week hands-on course at George Mason University's MIX to design, build, and test unmanned aerial vehicles. Working in a team of three, I assembled a functional drone from a kit, configured flight systems using ArduPilot, and completed multiple flight tests to ensure reliability and performance.

The program covered key UAV concepts such as power systems, flight controllers, PID control theory, and autonomous flight modes. Gained practical experience through hands-on testing, including bench tests and both indoor and outdoor flight trials, while developing skills in hardware assembly, software setup, and troubleshooting flight systems.

Drone Assembly & Hardware Integration

Built a fully operational UAV from a kit, handling everything from assembling the airframe to installing motors and ESCs, and ensuring structural integrity for safe flights. Personally hand-soldered all connections between the flight controller, receivers, ESCs, and servos, ensuring dependable performance and minimizing the chance of in-flight issues.

Paid close attention to wire organization and connection quality to avoid signal interference or power problems. This hands-on project sharpened my eye for detail in hardware assembly and boosted my skills in troubleshooting complex electrical systems.

ArduPilot Configuration & Flight Testing

Configured the UAV using ArduPilot open-source flight control software. Key tasks included:

  • Frame setup and motor calibration for flight stability
  • RC transmitter/receiver pairing and link establishment
  • SServo calibration for precise control surface actuation
  • Flight mode programming (Stabilize, AltHold, Autonomous)

I carried out systematic testing in progressive stages: starting with bench tests to confirm basic functionality, moving to controlled indoor flights to check stability, and finishing with detailed outdoor trials to evaluate overall performance. Along the way, I fine-tuned control parameters based on how the system performed, improving both its handling and reliability.

Tools & Skills

  • ArduPilot flight control software
  • Soldering and electrical integration
  • PID controller tuning
  • RC transmitter configuration
  • Flight testing and troubleshooting
  • Team collaboration and safety protocols

What I learned

Working on this project sharpened my skills in systems integration and highlighted how vital thorough testing is in complex engineering setups. I gained a stronger grasp of how hardware quality directly impacts system reliability and got hands-on experience refining designs through iteration. Collaborating with the team reinforced the value of clear communication and keeping safety front and center during prototyping.
