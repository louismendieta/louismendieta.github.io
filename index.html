A Little About Me

Hello, I'm Louis Mendieta, a rising junior studying mechanical engineering with a concentration in propulsion systems at Virginia Tech.

Throughout my undergraduate years, I've pursued hands-on opportunities in engineering research, design teams, internships, and work-studies while maintaining strong academic performance.

I seek out projects that push me beyond my comfort zone, aiming to build both technical expertise and leadership skills for graduate school and industry.
